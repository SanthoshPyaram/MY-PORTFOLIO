Key Capabilities & Architecture

  • Crop & Soil Analytics: Tracks soil moisture, environmental temperature, and nutrient metrics.

COOPNEX — Cooperative Services Platform

Project Overview

A digital cooperative services marketplace concept designed to connect workers, service providers, and customers through service discovery, worker profiles, booking, scheduling, and cooperative administration.

Key Capabilities & Architecture

  • Verified Worker Directory: Showcase certified cooperative tradespeople with skills, verified ratings, and transparent fees.
  • Automated Booking & Dispatch: Customers select available date/time slots with automated allocation.
  • Fair Cooperative Revenue Distribution: Transparent fee split allocating majority payout directly to workers alongside cooperative welfare funds.
  • Society Admin Portal: Real-time dashboard monitoring active bookings, member welfare metrics, and user feedback.
